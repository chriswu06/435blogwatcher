Privacy Policy: 435blogwatcher

435blogwatcher is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests access to Google Tasks (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) solely to create, read, and update tasks in a task list named "CMSC435" in the owner's own account.

  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The only data stored is the text of public course blog posts and the IDs of tasks the tool created, kept in the project's repository.
  • OAuth credentials are held privately by the owner and are never published.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
